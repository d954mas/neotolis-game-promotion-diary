--- title: Auto-import now covers six platforms date: 2026-06-14 summary: Register a channel once and the diary pulls in new posts and their stats — for YouTube, Reddit, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok and X. ---

Logging every promo by hand gets old fast. So the diary now imports for you: register a source once, and new posts show up in your feed with their numbers already attached.

The six

  • YouTube — videos from a channel, with view counts.
  • Reddit — your posts in a subreddit, with score and comments.
  • Telegram — posts from a public channel, with view counts.
  • Instagram — your posts, with likes and comments.
  • TikTok — your videos, with plays and engagement.
  • X (Twitter) — your tweets, with impressions and likes.

How it works

Add a source on the Sources page. From then on the diary checks it on a schedule, files anything new into your chronological feed, and keeps re-reading the stats so the numbers stay current. Tag each post with the game it promotes and it shows up on that game's wishlist chart — promo events and the wishlist line on one timeline.

Some platforms hand their data out for free; others need a paid scraper behind the scenes. Either way it stays within the indie budget the project is built for — no managed services in the critical path.
