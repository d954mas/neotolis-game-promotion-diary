The diary is live — a self-tracking log for indie game devs that answers one question: which of the things you did actually moved your wishlists?

What you can do right now:

  • Log any promo — YouTube, Reddit, Telegram, conferences, press — in one timeline.
  • Auto-import posts and their stats from six platforms: YouTube, Reddit, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok and X.
  • Tag a post to a game and watch it land on that game's wishlist chart.
  • Private by default — free hosted, or self-host it (MIT).

Sign in with Google and start logging. More platforms and polish are on the way.
