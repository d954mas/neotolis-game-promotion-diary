The diary is live — a self-tracking log for indie game devs that shows which of your promos actually moved your wishlists.

Log any promo by hand, or auto-import posts and their stats from six platforms:

  • YouTube
  • Reddit
  • Telegram
  • Instagram
  • TikTok
  • X

Tag a post to a game and watch it land on that game's wishlist chart. It's private by default — free hosted, or self-host it.

Sign in with Google and start logging. More platforms and polish are on the way.
