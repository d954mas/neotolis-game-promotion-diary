--- title: v0.1 is live date: 2026-05-04 summary: The promo diary is open — log every promotion, auto-import the stats, and watch what actually moves wishlists. ---

The first public version of the Neotolis promotion diary is out. It exists to answer one question every indie dev keeps re-asking: which of the things I did actually moved wishlists?

What it does

  • One chronological feed. Every promo action — a YouTube video, a Reddit thread, a Telegram post, a conference, a press email — lands in a single timeline you can scroll back through months later.
  • Stats that accumulate. Each entry carries its own numbers (views, reactions, comments) so you can see, side by side, what landed and what didn't.
  • Private by default. Your data is scoped to you. No public dashboards, no share links — it's a diary, not a billboard.

Getting started

Sign in with Google and start logging. There's nothing to configure and no card to enter. If you'd rather run it yourself, the whole thing is open source (MIT) and self-hosts on a small VPS — the hosted version and the self-host image are the same code.

More platforms and automatic imports are next. This is just the start.
